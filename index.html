Understanding Simple Random Sampling

Learn how Mr. O uses sampling to efficiently check homework without reviewing all 32 assignments!

📖 The Scenario

Mr. O is a teacher with 32 students in his class.

Instead of checking all 32 homework assignments, he decides to randomly check 10 assignments to estimate how many students completed their homework.

This is Simple Random Sampling - each student has an equal chance of being selected!
